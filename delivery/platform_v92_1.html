How Multisig Escrow Works
2-of-2 + arbitration
1
Match with trader
Accept an offer or create your own with terms & payment method
2
Fund 2-of-2 multisig
BTC locked in deposit address — neither party can withdraw alone
3
Pay via agreed method
Send fiat / stablecoin via SEPA, Revolut, Cash App, bank transfer…
4
Seller confirms & releases
BTC unlocked to buyer — trade complete in 5–15 min
!
Dispute?
Bisq: decentralized DAO arbitration. Hodl Hodl: 2-of-3 with mediator.
P2P Safety Checklist
› Never trade outside the escrow — no private deals
› Check trader rating & trade count before accepting
› Use payment methods the platform tracks for dispute evidence
› Keep chat inside the platform — screenshots as proof
› Start small — verify settlement before larger amounts
⚠ Scam rate industry-wide ~0.02% on escrow platforms — still stay vigilant
